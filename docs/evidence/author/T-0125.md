## What was delivered

- EDIT-008: `Token::FileName`, `PageSetContext`, `render_page_set`,
  `compose_page_set`, `Overlay::into_bytes`, and
  `PageSelection::Alternate { every, offset }` with a zero-stride typed error;
  all deterministic and copy-on-write.
- EDIT-009: `render_watermark_page_set` plus alternate-selection support in
  `Watermark::includes_page`; existing text/vector/image, rotation, opacity, and
  tiling paths retained.
- EDIT-011: new `navigation::edit` module with `BookmarkPath`,
  `Outline::{bookmark_at, insert_bookmark, rename_bookmark, remove_bookmark,
  move_bookmark}` (cycle rejection on descendant moves), `LinkStore`, and
  `NameTree::remove`, with seven new `NavigationError` variants.

## Notes

- No new dependencies, no `unsafe`, and no `unwrap`/`expect`/`panic!` in
  non-test code. All new public items are rustdoc'd; the crate keeps
  `#![deny(missing_docs)]` and `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`.
- "PDF-bytes output" is delivered at the crate's actual boundary: deterministic
  content-stream bytes plus resource patches. This crate has no PDF-writer and
  cannot emit complete PDF files; that remains a `pdfxi-core` responsibility.
- Optional `docs/book/src/authoring.md` edit was denied by lane permissions
  (that path is not in the author lane's `owns`/permission list). The crate-level
  rustdoc was updated instead; no blocker for the required deliverables.
