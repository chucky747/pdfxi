# T-0149 - forms property editing and signature planning

Lane: `forms`. Target dir: `target/lanes/forms` (then verified in
`target/integration`).

## Delivered

- `crates/pdfxi-forms/src/propertyedit.rs` (new): `PropertyEditor` publishes
  the properties that apply to each `FieldType`, collects typed `PropertyEdit`s
  into a deterministic `EditPlan`, validates the plan atomically, and applies it
  to the property set, the emitted field dictionaries and the in-memory form,
  then re-runs validation. An `EditSnapshot` supports undo.
- `crates/pdfxi-forms/src/signatureplan.rs` (new): `SignaturePlan` produces the
  exact `/FT /Sig` widget and `/V` placeholder contract expected by a signer
  (`/Contents` and `/ByteRange` deliberately absent); `PlaceholderContract`
  validates it without generating or forging material. A `SignatureGuard`
  derived from `/Lock` and DocMDP certification is installed into the fill
  workflow, blocking edits according to lock scope and certification level.
