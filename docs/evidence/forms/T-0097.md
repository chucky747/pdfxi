## What was delivered

- `crates/pdfxi-forms/src/acrofill.rs` (new): `fill_acroform`,
  `FieldAssignment`, `AcroFillError`, `AcroFillReport`, `FilledField`,
  `SkippedField`, `RejectedField`, `SkipReason`.
- Applies `/V` and optional `/DV`, derives widget `/AS`, regenerates `/AP /N`
  through the existing `build_appearance`, honors `/Ff` flags (read-only,
  required, radio, button), updates every widget of a multi-widget field
  consistently, resolves hierarchical names, and normalizes dates, numbers,
  currency, and choice index/export/display values deterministically.
- Deterministic name-sorted report; untouched widgets stay byte-stable.
- 17 new tests in `acrofill::tests`.

## Scope

The fill model is headless and copy-on-write; CLI/UI binding and FDF/XFDF import
remain other rows. Invalid geometry, invalid dates, read-only fields, and
unknown names produce typed or skipped outcomes instead of panics.
