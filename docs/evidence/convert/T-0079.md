## Coverage

22 new tests (49 total, up from 27). All ten required scenarios are covered:
PDF/A font embedding (`pdf_a_reports_unembedded_font`), PDF/X output intent
(`pdf_x_reports_missing_output_intent`), version mismatch
(`version_mismatch_is_reported`), transparency (`transparency_is_reported`),
encryption (`encryption_is_prohibited`), metadata fixup
(`add_xmp_metadata_fixup_is_opt_in_and_reported`), opt-in prohibited-entry fixup
(`remove_prohibited_entry_fixup_is_opt_in`), severity/order determinism
(`severity_and_order_are_deterministic`), clean document
(`clean_input_passes_all_named_profiles`), and fixup report accuracy
(`fixup_report_is_accurate`). Additional fixup tests cover typed errors,
version normalization, output-intent profile gating, idempotence, and report
drift from preflight suggestions.

Safety: no new dependencies; `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`; no `unwrap`, `expect`,
or `panic!` in non-test code. Fixups edit a cloned snapshot only, are disabled
by default, and are fully reported.

## Limits

Fixups do not rewrite PDF bytes — a producer adapter must apply the snapshot to
its object model. Output-intent and XMP additions are placeholders and do not by
themselves establish conformance. Full ICC validation, veraPDF CI comparison,
PDF/VT, and UI/CLI exposure remain future work.
