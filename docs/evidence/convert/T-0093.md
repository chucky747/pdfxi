## What was delivered

- `crates/pdfxi-standards/src/profile_request.rs` (new): `ProfileRequest`,
  `ConformanceProfile`, typed `ProfileRequestError` (`UnsupportedProfile`,
  `ContradictoryRequirements`), and deterministic dispatch onto the existing
  PDF/A-1b/2b, PDF/X-4, and PDF/E-1 profiles.
- `crates/pdfxi-standards/src/document_profile.rs`: transparency policy
  accessor plus 13 conformance tests covering conforming and violating fixtures
  for every profile, missing XMP, wrong version, missing output intent,
  encryption, forbidden JavaScript, transparency, deterministic rule IDs and
  ordering, page/object context, and non-mutating fixups.
- `crates/pdfxi-convert/src/conformance.rs` (new): `ConformanceTarget`,
  `ConformanceRequest`, `NormalizationOptions`, typed `ConformanceExportError`,
  a deterministic classic-xref reader/writer, XMP and output-intent/ICC
  placeholders, header version normalization, regenerated xref offsets for
  appended objects, a replacement catalog with a repointed `/Root`, and an
  honest `ConformanceExportReport` (`enforced`, `reported_only`,
  `certification_claimed() == false`, `SCOPE_NOTE`).
- `crates/pdfxi-convert/tests/conformance_export.rs` (new): three round-trip
  tests through `pdfxi-core`.

## Scope

Normalization enforces the structural subset it can verify (header version, XMP
presence, output intent entry, encryption refusal, forbidden actions) and
reports — without silently claiming — ICC validity, XMP content correctness, and
backend-dependent checks. No conformance certification is claimed.
