## What was delivered

- `crates/pdfxi-convert/src/office_export/` (new): deterministic stored-ZIP
  writer plus DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX assembly from a caller-supplied page/text
  model. Required OOXML parts are emitted; text is XML-escaped; content that the
  adapters cannot express is reported through a typed export report instead of
  being silently dropped.
- DOCX paragraphs/headings/lists/tables; XLSX sheet-per-page with typed cells;
  PPTX slide-per-page with positioned text boxes.
- No network, no Office installation, no new dependencies, no `unsafe`, no
  `unwrap`/`expect`/`panic!` in non-test code.
