Fresh-eyes review found that the human feature summaries contradicted the
canonical matrix, violating AC-04 ("`docs/FEATURES.md` and the generated matrix
agree"):

- `docs/FEATURES.md` said `54 verified, 13 library, 76 model, 2 replaced` and
  "91 rows are not verified".
