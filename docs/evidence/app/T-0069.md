## Reviewer-driven corrections

1. `PdfxiApp::close_other_tabs` previously called
   `state.close_other_tabs()` directly, bypassing `UiClosePolicy` despite the
   rustdoc. It now delegates to `close_other_tabs_with_policy` with the same hook
   used by the tab close button and `Ctrl+W`, and returns a typed
   `CloseOthersOutcome` (`closed` plus policy-`kept` ids). New test
   `ui::tests::close_others_policy_keeps_dirty_tabs` asserts a dirty tab is
   retained under `RequireClean` and closed after switching to `DiscardDirty`.
2. The new-test count was corrected: this task adds **18** tests (8 state, 2
   tabs, 8 ui), not 19. The earlier claim of 19 was an overcount.
3. `orchestration/feature-updates/T-0069.toml` was restored with the same
   VIEW-005 verified row, corrected counts, and the new test name. `features
   apply` was not run.
