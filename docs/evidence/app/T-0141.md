## Honest status

Both feature rows remain `in-progress` at the library/model level. The delivered
models fully implement the pure logic described by the tasks (document-ordered
slide sequence, transitions, durations, caller-timed auto-advance, commands,
deterministic layout; circular/rectangular lens, bounded zoom, page/screen
mapping, hit-testing, bounded gesture accumulation). They deliberately do not
enter an OS full-screen window, register native gestures, or render the loupe to
a real canvas — that integration is host/`ui` work. Per ADR 0008 point 3, a
partial local equivalent stays `in-progress` rather than being marked verified
or replaced.
