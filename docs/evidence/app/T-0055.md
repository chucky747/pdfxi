# T-0055 — Accessibility tree and reading-order export

Status: verified (task). ACCESS-001 and ACCESS-002 remain in-progress: this task
delivers the tree/export model, not the interactive editor UI or the full
PDF/UA checker.

## Files

- `crates/pdfxi-accessibility/src/tree.rs` (new) — `NodeId`, `BBox` (finite,
  positive extent), `A11yNode`, and `A11yTree` with deterministic depth-first
  traversal. Validation rejects duplicate ids, unknown children or roots,
  multiple parents, cycles (iterative three-colour DFS, including disconnected
  cycles), orphan nodes, and non-finite/empty boxes. Font-size validation lives
  in `builder.rs` (`TextBlock::new` and `ReadingOrderBuilder::build`), not here.
- `crates/pdfxi-accessibility/src/builder.rs` (new) — `TextBlock` and
  `ReadingOrderBuilder` with canonical sort, same-line merging, vertical-gap
  paragraph splitting, font-size heading detection, and stable ids.
- `crates/pdfxi-accessibility/src/export.rs` (new) — `to_plain_text` in reading
  order with role labels and indentation, plus `summarize` with role counts and
  alt-text coverage.
- `crates/pdfxi-accessibility/src/lib.rs` — `TagRole::Page`, `Ord` derive, module
  wiring, and re-exports.

No new dependencies; `.opencode` permission mismatch for the app lane was
reported to the integrator.
