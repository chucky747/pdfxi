## Files

  and a `pdfium` feature; `default = []` unchanged.
- `crates/pdfxi-app/src/raster.rs` — dependency-light `RasterCache` keyed by
  `(page, scale_bits, rotation)` with a 64-entry LRU, `ensure_visible`, `get`,
  `put`, invalidation, cancellation, and structured errors.
- `crates/pdfxi-app/src/ui.rs` — with `pdfium`, opens `fixtures/minimal.pdf`,
  renders visible pages, uploads the active raster to a reused egui texture, and
  paints it inside the canvas page rectangle; without `pdfium`, the placeholder
  remains.
