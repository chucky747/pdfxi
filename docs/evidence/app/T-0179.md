# T-0179 - app lane reflow, compare, portfolio management and shared folders

Lane: `app`. Target dir: `target/lanes/app`, re-run by the integrator in
`target/integration`. Date: 2026-11-19.

## Delivered (crates/pdfxi-app/** only)

- `reflow.rs` (VIEW-013): paragraph segmentation on blank lines with soft-break
  preservation, `ReflowParagraph`, `paragraph_index` per line and a
  `paragraphs()` accessor; deterministic per-paragraph wrapping.
- `read_aloud.rs` (VIEW-013): `Cadence`, `Utterance`, `UtteranceQueue`,
  `TickOutcome`, typed `ReadAloudError`; deterministic reading-order utterance
  queue, fixed-point millisecond timing with remainder carry, pause/resume/seek.
- `compare_view.rs` (VIEW-015): `PagePairKind`, `PagePair`, `PagePairSummary`,
  `PagePairing` (aligned/inserted/deleted), `CompareViewModel::page_pairing`,
  typed `InvalidPageNumber`/`NonMonotonicPages`, byte-stable summary.
- `portfolio_manage.rs` (MANAGE-004): `ManageOp::Describe`,
  `PortfolioManager::describe`, `ManagePlan::parse` as the exact inverse of
  `serialize`, typed `InvalidDescription`/`InvalidPlan`.
- `shared_folder.rs` (MANAGE-006): validated `SharedFolderPath` /
  `SharedFolderSource`, bounded listing plan, `SyncItem`, `WorkspaceDescriptor`,
  `ConflictKind`, `SyncConflict`, `ConflictReport`, `SyncAction`, `SyncStep`,
  `SyncPlan` with deterministic serialization; traversal, absolute-outside-root,
  unsafe names, duplicate items and revision overflow are typed errors.
- `lib.rs`: re-exports and rustdoc.

23 new tests (requirement was at least 16).
