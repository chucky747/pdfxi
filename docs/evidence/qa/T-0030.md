## Notes

The 500-page fixture is 145,516 bytes, far under the 2 MiB cap. Open measures a
real `Document::from_bytes` parse; first-page measures reaching page 0 and
extracting its text; all-pages measures a full text pass. These numbers are the
M1 exit evidence for `docs/PLAN.md`.
