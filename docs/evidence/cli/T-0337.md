# T-0337 — Separations and output-preview host (`PRINT-004`)

Row promoted: `PRINT-004` (Output preview and separations) `model` ->
`verified`.

`pdfxi_app::print::SeparationsPlan` owned the canonical ink list but nothing
derived the inks from a document. This task adds `pdfxi separations`, which
scans real content streams for colour operators, resolves `/Separation` and
`/DeviceN` resources, counts paint operations per plate and answers the
output-preview question for one plate.

## 1. Host

```text
pdfxi separations <INPUT.pdf> [--plate NAME] [--json]
```

- Decodes every page content stream and tokenizes it (skipping strings,
  comments and inline-image data).
- Tracks the graphics state (`q`/`Q`) for fill/stroke colour spaces.
- Resolves named `/ColorSpace` resources from the page's inherited
  `/Resources`, including `/Separation (Spot name)` and `/DeviceN [names]`.
- Counts paint operations for `k`/`K` (all four process inks), `g`/`G`
  (black), `rg`/`RG` (device RGB, reported separately), `sh` and
  `sc`/`scn`/`SC`/`SCN` under a spot/DeviceN space.
- Builds `SeparationsPlan::build` from the discovered inks (process names are
  marked process, everything else is a spot) and prints ids, process/spot,
  operations and pages.
- `--plate NAME` (case-insensitive) is the output preview for one ink.

## 2. Evidence

Fixture: one page whose content is
`q /CS0 cs 1 scn Q 0.1 0.2 0.3 0.4 k 0.5 0.6 0.7 rg 0.4 g`, with
`/ColorSpace << /CS0 [/Separation (Spot Orange) /DeviceCMYK 5 0 R] >>` and a
type-2 tint function.

```text
> pdfxi separations target\tmp\separations.pdf
file: target\tmp\separations.pdf  pages: 1  device-rgb-ops: 1  device-gray-ops: 1
separations: 5 total, 4 process, 1 spot
  1  process  Black  2 op(s)  pages [1]
  2  process  Cyan  1 op(s)  pages [1]
  3  process  Magenta  1 op(s)  pages [1]
  4  process  Yellow  1 op(s)  pages [1]
  5  spot  Spot Orange  1 op(s)  pages [1]
descriptor: separations-plan/v1|1:process:2:Black|2:process:1:Cyan|3:process:1:Magenta|4:process:1:Yellow|5:spot:1:Spot Orange
EXIT=0

> pdfxi separations target\tmp\separations.pdf --plate "Spot Orange"
plate: Spot Orange  process: false  operations: 1  pages: [1]

> pdfxi separations target\tmp\separations.pdf --json
"plates": [ {"id":1,"name":"Black","process":true,"operations":2,"pages":[1]}, ...,
            {"id":5,"name":"Spot Orange","process":false,"operations":1,"pages":[1]} ]
"summary": "separations: 5 total, 4 process, 1 spot"
```

Rejections:

```text
> pdfxi separations FILE --plate Varnish
Error: no plate named "Varnish"
```

A page with only `q Q` reports no plates and `separations: none`.

## Scope

No separation proofing, plate rasterization, ink-limit computation, trapping or
file output. Colour conversion follows the document's declared spaces only;
device RGB is counted but not mapped to process plates. `/Separation` and
`/DeviceN` names are read verbatim; ICCBased and Indexed spaces are reported as
other and do not create plates.
