  form submission/import actions are rejected as dangerous; URI, remote go-to
  and multimedia actions are rejected as unsupported. No script is executed.

  host integration, MEDIA-001, MEDIA-002 and MEDIA-005 are reported as
  `in-progress` (matching the T-0134 precedent for MEDIA-003/MEDIA-004), not
  `verified` or `replaced`.
