## Blocker

**T-0023 — unbounded predictor allocation.** `apply_predictor` computed the row
length from attacker-controlled `/DecodeParms` (`/Columns`, `/Colors`,
`/BitsPerComponent`) and allocated `vec![0u8; row_length]` without bounding it
by the decoded stream. A tiny valid zlib stream with a huge `/Columns` could
force a multi-gigabyte allocation or capacity-overflow panic.

Resolution: the core lane bounds the row allocation by the decoded length
(rejecting `row_length > data.len()` with a structured error) and adds a hostile
`/DecodeParms` regression test. Tracked in T-0031 follow-up and its fragment.

## Minor findings

- T-0025: zero-dimension PNGs are accepted by the encoder; unreachable from the
  render path but not spec-conformant. Fix deferred until an image API stabilizes.
- T-0026: `SearchOptions::whole_word` is only honored by `SearchIndex::find`,
  not the legacy single-page `search`; documented. `SearchMatch::len()` /
  `IndexMatch::len()` can underflow if a caller constructs `start > end`; to be
  hardened with `saturating_sub` and constructor validation.
- T-0027: selection head is exclusive; consistent and tested, worth stating in
  user docs.
